Palak Paneer Pasta

Indian-Italian 🇮🇳🇮🇹 Published 19 July 2026
Journal Notes
  • Prepared: 14 June 2026
  • Cuisine: Indian-Italian 🇮🇳🇮🇹
  • Difficulty: 🔥🔥🔥💧💧
  • Servings: 2

🍜 Why I Made It

I made Palak Paneer once and I also make pasta many a times. I heard that the former dish can go well with rice, roti/naan/chapati, or even noodles. I thought to myself, why not as a rich sauce for pasta? And so, an attempt hath been made.

🛒 Ingredients

Main

  • 250 g paneer cubes
  • 250–300 g spinach (palak)
  • 250 g pasta

Aromatics

  • 1 medium onion, sliced
  • 5–6 garlic cloves
  • 1 inch ginger
  • 1 green chili

Flavor Balancers

  • 1 medium tomato
  • 3 tbsp milk
  • 1 tsp kasuri methi
  • ½ tsp sugar or honey
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • 6–8 cashews
  • Cheese

Spices

  • ½ tsp cumin seeds
  • ½ tsp coriander powder
  • ¼ tsp garam masala
  • ¼ tsp black pepper
  • Salt to taste

👨‍🍳 Method

  1. Blanch the Spinach

    Add spinach to boiling water for only 45–60 seconds and immediately transfer to ice-cold water.

  2. Cook the pasta

    • Boil water and dissolve generous amounts of salt in it.
    • Cook 250g of pasta (spiral, penne, whatever) until almost al dente.
    • Reserve ½ cup pasta water before draining.

  3. Make the Smooth Green Base

    • Blend blanched spinach, tomato, cashews, and green chili together.
    • If needed, add a little water.

  4. Build Flavor

    • Heat 1 tbsp butter and a little oil in a pan.
    • Throw in cumin seeds and onions.
    • Cook the onions until lightly golden.
    • Add ginger and garlic. Cook for 1 minute until fragrant.
    • Now add coriander powder and black pepper.

  5. Add the Spinach Puree

    • Add the puree and simmer only for 3-4 minutes.
    • Add salt, sugar/honey, kasuri methi, and cream/milk.

  6. Paneer and pasta go in last

    • Add paneer cubes and the pasta we cooked earlier.
    • Simmer gently and adjust for taste. Don't simmer it for more than 3 minutes as the spinach taste will become stronger the more you cook it.
    • Splash some reserved pasta water if it looks a little too dry to make it saucey.
    • Top it with lots of cheese. Mix it in and make it creamy.

✅ What Went Well?

Everything. This was just an experiment so I didn't have such expectations, and yet, surprisingly, it came out to be an amazing dish.

🔧 What I'd Change Next Time

The cheese I added or something else made the gravy very rich and heavy. Although it was delicious, it was tough to finish the bowl. I was already full by then. (I did take seconds.) I think I'll need to figure out what made it so filling and then, adjust it to make it a lighter dish as I'd like it to be.

🤔 Would I Make It Again?

Yes, but only when I figure out a hypothesis to make a lighter version of it. Not really excited to make it again though as I'd like to try making many different pastas before revisiting this one.
